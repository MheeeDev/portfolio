## Problem

A large public hospital, which was also installing rooftop solar, wanted to know where its
electricity went and what it cost. The utility bill gave one figure for the whole site; nobody could
say which building used what, or what that use was costing.

## My role & scope

My part was the monitoring and the money: setting up energy monitoring in Siemens Energy Manager
PRO from the SENTRON power meters in the distribution boards, and calculating the electricity
cost from the measured energy.

## Approach

### Measured, not estimated

The PAC5200 meters in the distribution boards report voltage, current, power and energy for each
building. Energy Manager PRO stores those readings and shows them as dashboards of use per
building, with trends and gauges.

### From kWh to baht

The energy readings are turned into an electricity cost, so the facilities team sees what each
building's use is costing, not only how many kilowatt-hours it took.

## Delivery

- Energy monitoring configured in Energy Manager PRO for the meters on site
- Electricity cost calculation from the measured energy
- Walk-through with the hospital's engineers at handover

## Result

- Electricity use and cost visible per building instead of one monthly figure
- A measured baseline for the hospital's energy-saving work and its solar plant
