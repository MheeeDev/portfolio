## Problem

A vegetable-oil plant packs what is left after oil extraction — meal and hulls — into big bags of
about 1.35 t each, to be sold as animal feed. The weighing and handling equipment came from a
specialist vendor, and the plant wanted a control system that was more modern and that its own
team could follow: one sequence, one operator screen, clear signals to the machines around it.

## My role & scope

I wrote the PLC program from scratch and designed the HMI for the line, on the integrator's team:

- **Sequence** — buffer hopper, feed valves, mini screw, weigh hopper, slide gate, dust collector,
  conveyors and pallet handling, written as flowcharts first and reviewed by the customer
- **Weighing** — tare, three feed speeds and a ±0.7% tolerance window against the set point
- **Signal exchange** — hardwired signals with the plant's cabinet, S7 PUT/GET with the bag-station PLC
- **HMI** — a one-page process overview on a TP1200 Comfort, plus a 12-step operating guide

## Approach

- **Weigh first, fill once.** Material is weighed in a 3 m³ hopper on load cells, where the reading is
  steady, then dumped into the bag through a slide gate. When the scale reads zero, the gate closes
  after 2 s and the station gets *Filling Finish*.
- **Slow down before the target.** The screw runs FAST to SP1, SLOW 1 to SP2 and SLOW 2 to the set
  point, so the material still in the air when the valves close stays small.
- **Every signal has an owner.** Five signals each way with the plant and bag station (Valve Feed
  Open, Big Bag Ready, Start Filling, Start to Transfer, Pallet Supply Ready ↔ Auto Mode Run, Safety
  OK, Weigh Finish, Filling Finish, Pallet Empty). Safety-related ones are hardwired; the bag-station
  data block carries a toggling health bit both ways and raises *Connection Fail* when it stops.
- **Alarms agreed before code.** Safety/emergency, valve fault (no limit-switch feedback), breaker,
  VSD, connection fail and tare-not-ready, each acknowledged on the HMI. An open isolator takes its
  motor out of auto and shows a red *Isolate* state.

## Delivery

- Kick-off meeting in mid-May 2024, then flowcharts and text files for the hull and meal lines
- TIA Portal V17 versions from late May to mid-August 2024, with an I/O check list against the panels
- Commissioning plan: a 3–4 day dry test, then a trial with real product

## Result

- One program and one operator screen for the line, from the buffer hopper to the empty pallet
- Each bag weighed in the hopper and filled in one dump, with set point and actual weight on screen
- Stops show which signal or device is missing, instead of a line that silently waits

The full story in Thai, with the interactive weigh cycle and the signal table, is in the
